# Dusk Raven Works — visual identity

## Accepted creative direction

**Subject:** A single raven perched on a dark tree branch, foregrounded against an exceptionally large luminous moon.

**Atmosphere:** Purple/indigo night with restrained rose and lavender moonlight; still, contemplative, mysterious, beautiful. Secondary landscape forms are optional. The raven, moon, and branch are the primary symbols.

**Naming:** *Dusk Raven Works* is the full publisher name, with *Dusk Raven* acceptable as a short reference. The production/industrial quality belongs to the word *Works* and to deliberate craft, not necessarily to gears or buildings in the artwork.
