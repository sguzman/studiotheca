# Visual-system specification — working v0.1

## Hierarchy

1. **Moon:** The dominant luminous circular mass behind the foreground subject.
2. **Raven:** A legible single bird, perched, not in flight.
3. **Branch:** Distinct perch and near-field framing; can enter from an edge.
4. **Atmosphere:** Violet dusk/night, subtle clouds and stars.
5. **Optional environment:** Distant landscape, forest silhouettes, reflective water, mist.

The preferred artwork uses a mostly dark raven and branch, with restrained edge highlights. Lighting, depth, and negative space should direct attention without losing the bird against the moon.

The canonical symbolic relation is **raven perched on branch against moon in violet twilight**. An asset may simplify scenery and color while preserving recognizable symbols.

## Formats and reduction

- **Cinematic scene:** Allows mountain/lake/cloud layers and moon surface detail.
- **Small icon:** Eliminate distant scenery first; preserve black raven silhouette, moon disc, and essential branch.
- **Single-color:** Favor the raven's beak, head, body, claw, and branch silhouettes; moon may be a negative-space circle.
- **Wordmark:** Do not bake the name into detailed scene art unless the text remains independently readable.
- **Motion:** Optional future clouds, light shifts, feather motion; produce a still equivalent. No animation is yet approved.

## Exclusions

Do not conflate a raven with a heron, crow, wolf or stag in the main identity. Do not add mandatory cogwheels, factories, or smoke. Do not invent a specific typographic family, logo lockup, or precisely sampled palette without a recorded review.
