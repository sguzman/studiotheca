# Violet moon — raven on branch

**ID:** violet-moon  
**Kind:** founding scene / composition  
**Status:** Positive artistic reception; candidate, not commercial master.  
**First version:** [v001 — giant moon and perched raven](versions/v001/README.md).

## Hypothesis

A single large dark raven on a twisted tree branch, seen against an extraordinary lavender moon and purple twilight environment, creates a memorable publisher identity that is contemplative rather than aggressively industrial.

## Fixed symbols

Raven, perch/branch, exceptionally large moon, violet sky. Distant mountains and water may be interpreted differently or removed in a simplified mark.

## Version policy

The v001 PNG is a raster original with a known checksum. Changing composition, colors, wording, or subject requires a separate version or experiment; do not overwrite the recorded first image.
