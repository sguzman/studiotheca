# Typography and wordmark — open specification

**Text to display:** `Dusk Raven Works`. Acceptable editorial shorthand: `Dusk Raven`; the official full name must remain available in every full publisher lockup.

## Desired character

Elegant, legible, atmospheric, and credible as a maker of games. The word *Works* should communicate disciplined production while the moon/raven image carries romance and mystery.

Candidate type directions to *test*, not prematurely select: restrained humanist serif, sharp transitional serif, or refined sans-serif with generous spacing. No font family has been accepted or purchased, and no font binary is included.

## Required test matrix

- Full wordmark on dark and light backgrounds.
- Horizontal and stacked lockups, with and without the emblem.
- Readable text at storefront thumbnail widths and 100% zoom.
- Authentic licensed font versus independent lettering; no unlicensed redistributed fonts.
- Legible letter spacing at small sizes; no faux-industrial cuts forced into type.
