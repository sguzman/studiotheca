# Typography — Onyx Foundry

## Primary: Berenis ADF Pro

**Decision:** Retain the **original unmodified glyphs**. Previous experiments involving machined, signature, or decorative letter cuts were rejected. Do not reintroduce them without an explicit new request.

**Display arrangement:** `ONYX` prominent; `FOUNDRY` smaller and deliberately letter-spaced. Use pale-stone solid matte text (working value `#D5CEC3`). Emblem materials can be richly textured; the letters should not all be shiny or over-textured.

**Asset policy:** Save a font specimen image/export when available, with a source and terms. Do not embed or redistribute font binaries in this archive without a rights check.

**Future experiments:** Kerning and wordmark size/layout are fair game; preserve the original outlines.
