# Integrated OF — industrial emblem

**Design thesis:** The carved wooden structure itself forms the F, with attached industrial elements forming its arms.

- **O:** Matte cracked onyx ring.
- **F spine:** Carved walnut post and obsidian-edged sides, with a blunt base.
- **Upper arm:** Right-facing metal barrel emitting smoke at the muzzle.
- **Upper joint:** Metal gear, a potential animation element.
- **Middle arm:** Metallic cog/gear.
- **Wordmark:** Unmodified Berenis ADF Pro letters and restrained sapphire accents.

[v001 — two-gear candidate](versions/v001/README.md) is the preferred recorded static direction (October 19, 2026). The original render has not been imported. Future smoke and gear-motion experiments remain unproduced. This is not a final commercial logo.
