# Integrated OF — steampunk mechanics

**Hypothesis:** The central wooden macuahuitl should itself form the F, so every major part of the emblem has structural meaning.

## Design components

- **O:** matte cracked onyx ring.
- **F spine:** brown carved wooden post, obsidian edge blocks, blunt lower end.
- **Top F arm:** horizontal aged-metal steampunk barrel, extending right and emitting smoke from its muzzle.
- **Upper joint:** visible metallic gear at the barrel's attachment point; intended to spin in eventual animations.
- **Middle F arm:** metallic cog/gear replacing the earlier wooden-and-onyx arm.
- **Wordmark:** unmodified Berenis ADF Pro, matte pale stone; sapphire diamond accents.

## Versions

- [v001 — two-gear candidate](versions/v001/README.md) — latest preferred direction (October 19, 2026). **Art file not yet imported.**

## Evolution and decisions

- The earlier center post felt decorative; it was promoted to the F's actual spine.
- Explored middle arms: forged metal, wooden/metal cap, obsidian-edged, minimal, wooden gear with onyx tips.
- Moved the middle arm upward one notch.
- Added an upper gear, then replaced a redundant vertical chimney with a single horizontal smoking steampunk barrel.
- Replaced the middle-arm beam with a full gear. User expressed preference for this latest composition.
- Future motion idea: gears rotate, smoke billows. Not generated or approved for this revised emblem yet.

**Do not mark this as the final canonical emblem without a separate approval.**
