## Current identity direction

**Prestige + industry + Mesoamerican material influences.** Matte black onyx ring (O), walnut wooden macuahuitl-like central structure (F), obsidian teeth, forged industrial mechanical arms, restrained sapphire accents.

Latest favorite visual concept: the horizontal smoking steampunk barrel forming the top arm of the F, a metal gear as its middle arm, and a gear joint above the walnut stem. It is a **preferred candidate**, not a fully cleared, final commercial logo.

The independent **32-bit animated ident** was positively received and can remain approved as that treatment even while the primary emblem evolves.
