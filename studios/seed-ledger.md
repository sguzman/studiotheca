# Studio identity seed ledger

**Status:** Parked concepts only. **Recorded:** 2026-10-26.

This ledger preserves studio-name and identity directions that are worth remembering but have **not** been selected for development. An entry here is not an established studio, production commitment, approved logo, available account handle, registered trademark, or commercially cleared name.

## Orivellum

- **State:** Seed — undeveloped and unselected.
- **Character:** Elegant, intellectual, otherworldly.
- **Creative premise:** Discovery, imagination, and unusual worlds; a publisher identity suited to imaginative and exploratory games.
- **Potential visual language:** Celestial geometry, ivory and midnight blue, refined typography, abstract astronomical emblems.
- **Notes:** This is a mood and identity hypothesis, not a finished brand design.

## Duskheron

- **State:** Seed — undeveloped and unselected.
- **Character:** Atmospheric, melancholic, artistic.
- **Creative premise:** Evocative worlds and emotionally resonant interactive experiences.
- **Potential visual language:** A heron silhouette, receding or fading horizon, muted violet, silver, and amber.
- **Notes:** The Duskheron identity remains an undeveloped seed. Its night-and-moon mood inspired the **separate** [Dusk Raven Works concept](dusk-raven-works/README.md). Duskheron itself remains intact and has not been renamed.

## Morrowspindle

- **State:** Seed — undeveloped and unselected.
- **Character:** Inventive, mechanical, whimsical.
- **Creative premise:** Experimentation, interlocking systems, and surprising interactions.
- **Potential visual language:** Delicate clockwork, copper, sea green, cream, and a kinetic emblem.
- **Notes:** The mechanical motif is an exploratory visual direction, not a constraint on games or a developed brand.

## Preservation and boundaries

- These are **name-and-mood seeds**. Do not automatically instantiate full studio dossiers, game catalogs, design task queues, graphics, or branding experiments from them.
- No selected name or priority is implied by their order in this ledger.
- Naming conflicts, platform handles, domain availability, and trademark clearance are **unverified** for adoption purposes; revisit those only if a seed is deliberately developed.
- Reference or mood imagery used during brainstorming is **not** included here and should not be treated as original commissioned branding.
- [Onyx Foundry](onyx-foundry/README.md) is a separate documented studio concept with its own history. This ledger does not rename, replace, or supersede it.

Further seeds can be appended here without changing their state or promoting them to developed studio records.
