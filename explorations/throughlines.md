# Cross-studio creative throughlines

These are reusable artistic goals, not genre constraints or mandatory features.

## Emotionally serious work

Aim for experiences whose emotional stakes are treated with care. "Serious" does not require unrelenting gloom, and does not determine genre.

## Rigorous systems

Prefer coherent, interacting, testable mechanics rather than decorative complexity. Rules should reward understanding and should meaningfully affect the player's experience.

## Variety of genres

A shared studio identity need not make every game feel or play the same. Action, simulation, narrative, strategy, and experimental forms are all possible.

## Prestige × industry (Onyx Foundry's identity, not a universal gameplay constraint)

The studio visual identity combines classical prestige, craftsmanship, heavy industry, onyx and obsidian, walnut wood, and restrained sapphire. This need not become the art style of every game.

## Evolving principles

A throughline should name the studio(s) and games that deliberately use it. Add examples, counterexamples, and revised statements as real projects develop. Do not retroactively describe brainstorming as firm creative doctrine.
