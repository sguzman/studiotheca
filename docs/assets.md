# Asset standards and rights

## Versioning

An experiment stores its overview in `README.md` and preserves individual studies under numbered `versions/vNNN/` directories. Source originals, rendered exports, and presentation previews can occupy distinct subdirectories if those files actually exist. New variants are new versions, not silent replacements.

## Provenance

For each imported image, animation, or other asset, record creator or tool, approximate date, source identifier, license/permissions, original format, dimensions or duration, and whether the exact file was verified. A generated raster is not an editable vector master. Pending entries in `media-import.yml` do not count as imported assets.

Do not embed broken galleries or distribute font binaries, textures, third-party artwork, or other files without rights review. The presence of a font name is not a redistribution grant.

## License and names

The existing [MIT license](../LICENSE) remains in place for Studiotheca's original material. Studio concepts, names, and emblem proposals are not represented as commercially cleared trademarks. Permissive copyright licensing and prevention of public impersonation are distinct matters. Uncertain third-party material must be excluded until it is cleared.
